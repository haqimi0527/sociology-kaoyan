一、建议信
挖空模板
Dear ...,
I am ... who... . I am writing this letter to express my humble opinions about ...
To begin with... In addition... Finally...
It is hoped that my suggestions will be of help and I am looking forward to your
early reply.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
背诵语料
1.
（开头）I am writing to propose some useful recommendations to you as
regards…
我写信是想就……向您提出一些有用的建议。
2.
（结尾）I trust you will take my suggestions into consideration.
我相信你会考
虑我的建议
3.
（中间）Firstly, it would be wise to join a study group, as collaborative learning
enhances understanding and motivation. 首先，加入一个学习小组是明智之举，
因为协作学习能增进理解并提升动力。
4.
（中间）Secondly, I strongly advise setting daily achievable goals—for instance,
reviewing one chapter per day—to avoid last-minute cramming. 其次，我强烈建
议设定每日可实现的目标——例如每天复习一章——以避免临时抱佛脚。
5.
（中间）Lastly, regular exercise is essential because it reduces stress hormones
and improves concentration, which directly benefits academic performance. 最
后，定期锻炼至关重要，因为它能降低压力荷尔蒙并提高专注力，从而直接
提升学业表现。

范文

I am a senior student at the university. I am writing this letter to express my
humble opinions about students’
physical condition.

physical education classes, integrating a range of activities that cater to students’

early reply.

that works better or get a full refund.
I hope you can deal with my complaint seriously. Thank you for your time and
consideration. Looking forward to your earliest reply.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

Minutes of our class meeting "Contributing to Aiding Rural Primary School project"
Time: From 10:00 a.m. to 12 a.m. on December 17, 2022
Place: Room 102, the Students’ Recreational Center
Present: All members of the Students’ Union
Presided by: Zhang Wei
Summary of the meeting：
I am attaching the minutes of the last class meeting for your review.The
summary of the meeting on contributing to the Aiding Rural Primary School project is
as follows.
Firstly, moved by the noble cause of this project and encouraged by what has
been achieved so far, we agreed to contribute our bit to the project by offering
financial aid to a child in a remote area of Yunnan Province. Secondly, we would seek
out a girl who has just started schooling but whose family cannot afford her education.
Last but not least, our plan
is to pay for her tuition on an annual basis until she
finishes her secondary education.We would like to remit our donation directly to a
bank account opened by her family in her local area.

“为希望工程做贡献”的班会纪要
时间:2022 年12 月17 日上午10 点至12 点
地点:学生活动中心102 室
主持人:张伟
会议纪要
现将上次班会的会议纪要附上以便大家审核。关于为希望工程做贡献的会议
纪要如下。
首先，“帮助农村小学”项目这项高尚的事业感动了我们，同时这个项目迄今
所取得的成就也鼓舞了我们。因此，我们同意通过给云南省偏远地区的一个孩
子提供经济帮助，来为希望工程贡献我们的微薄之力。其次，我们将找到一个刚
开始上学但家庭无法为其负担学费的小女孩。最后，我们计划按年度支付她的学
费直至中学毕业。我们将把捐款直接汇给她的家人在当地开设的银行账户中。
如果大家发现会议纪要中有错误或遗漏，请指正。
